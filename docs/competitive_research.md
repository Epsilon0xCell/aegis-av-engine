# Competitive research summary (condensed)

Full research: commercial engine decomposition, AV-Comparatives
Malware Protection Test September 2026 (10,000 samples:
Bitdefender 99.98 percent protection and 1 FP, Kaspersky 99.97
percent and 5 FP, Defender 99.89 percent and 28 FP, Malwarebytes
99.65 percent and 32 FP), platform constraints per OS, and the
nine niche wins this project targets (offline determinism,
throughput on a defined corpus, explainable verdicts, published
clean-corpus FP control, cross-OS consistency, zero telemetry,
footprint, ATT&CK fixture detection, script-fixture detection).

Standing rule, from that research: Aegis competes on the measured
axes in BENCHMARK.md and nowhere else. General-protection
comparisons with commercial engines are prohibited claims in this
project, because the corpora and conditions differ categorically.
Vendor names are used nominatively, for factual comparison of
published information only; no affiliation is implied.
